Distinguished Guests, Prospective Donors, Partners, and Friends,

Welcome to a defining moment in our history. Today, we stand at the threshold of a movement that will transition the plight of people with disabilities in Nigeria and Africa from the dark ages into a modern reality.

We are unveiling a scientifically backed, evidence-based revolution. For three years officially, SID Africa has practiced and gathered irrefutable physical evidence and testimonies. Backed by international research studies, statistics, and data from neurologists, we have proven that water is not just a leisure resource, it is a tool for neurological development, physical therapy, and social integration, spanning health, education and life preservation. Please read the scientific blog article and presentation I have prepared for the Doctors at Igbobi Orthopedic Hospital, Lagos, Nigeria. It presents overwhelming evidence confirming the points mentioned above.

Read the blog article for medical professionals

We have over 1,200 children with disabilities on our waiting list. We are here to ensure they are no longer waiting.

The dream that will be made reality with your support is to expand access to swimming, water safety, therapy, and adaptive swimming programmes for children and young people with disabilities in Africa for a better quality of life and more secure inclusive future. Our goal is bold: to raise a minimum of 50 Billion Naira annually that will unlock rivers of hope for Africa's most marginalized and forgotten.

What your donations will achieve: the blueprint

We are not just asking for funds; we are investing in infrastructure, safety, and the future. The funds raised will be deployed into the following critical areas.

1. SID Staff Training & Recruitment Boot Camp

To address the lack of specialized care, we will host certification bootcamps for:

  • Specialized Adaptive Swim Instructors & Water Therapists.
  • First Responders & Lifeguards.
  • Endurance & Open Water Coaches.
  • Safety & Trauma-Informed Coaching.
  • Media Campaigns: "What a Qualified Swim Coach Should Know" (#AskYourCoach).

The objective: to produce a minimum of 30 SID staff per year. This will be televised in a format similar to "Big Brother," showcasing the rigorous training required to save lives.

Why sponsor? Safety reform, job creation (SDG 4 & 8), and quality assurance.

2. Direct Care & Therapy for 1,200+ Children

  • Consistent Therapy: Giving specialized therapy for as long as necessary.
  • Combined Therapy: Integrating swim therapy with speech therapy or physiotherapy for children who need it to unlock speech and movement much faster.
  • Sea Therapy Allowances: Funding wholeness healing trips to the beach for our children.

3. Logistics & Operations

  • Commuter Electric and Solar Bus: To provide eco-friendly and reliable pick-up and drop-off for the kids.
  • Temporary Office Complex: Renting a facility with a standard gym, staff lodging, and camp facilities for holiday workshops (Summer Camps) for boys and girls.
  • Equipment: Laptops, printers, and advanced first aid kits (Mobile Clinic).
  • Underwater Cameras: To document, showcase and tell our stories better to the world.

4. The Future: A World-Class Facility

The Legacy Project: World Class Wellness Aquatics & Swimming Academy. We are commencing the search for land to build a first-of-its-kind World Class Wellness Aquatics & Swimming Academy in Nigeria. This facility, serving all of West Africa, will feature boarding facilities and an African-themed Water Park that will serve as a revenue-generating facility that ensures long-term sustainability.

To put our ambition in perspective, the Aquatics Olympic Centre in Paris 2024 cost roughly €180 million ($195M USD / 271.8 Billion Naira). We are building a legacy for Africa.

5. International Exchange & Knowledge

Special budget allocations for SID programs and international exchange programs to bring global standards to Nigeria.
